Dr Claudia Coleine was a collaborating member of the Stajich Lab from 2016 and joined the group as a Marie Skłodowska-Curie postdoc in 2023 to work on endolithic microbes from drylands. Her research spans fungi and microbes from extreme cold and/or dry environments from Antarctica to the Arctic.
