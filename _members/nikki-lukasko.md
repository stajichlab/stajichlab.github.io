Nikki is a postdoctoral scholar in the Department of Microbiology & Plant Pathology at UC Riverside advised by Dr. Alex Putnam and Dr. Jason Stajich. They received their Ph.D. in Plant Pathology at Michigan State. They work on projects related to the genomics, population genetics and disease pathobiology of Fusarium wilt in Lettuce.
