[Juhita Dhar](/members/juhita-dhar), [Prince Amoah](/members/prince-amoah), and [Jason Stajich](/members/jason-stajich) are co-authors, with Nicole Loeven and Deborah Hogan, on a new paper in _Microbiology Resource Announcements_: [Draft genome sequence of basidiomycete yeast _Rhodotorula mucilaginosa_ DH4148 from a patient with fungemia](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/mra.00821-26).

_Rhodotorula mucilaginosa_ is a basidiomycete yeast with ecological importance and growing clinical relevance. The paper reports a high-quality de novo genome assembly of strain DH4148, which was isolated from a patient with fungemia.
