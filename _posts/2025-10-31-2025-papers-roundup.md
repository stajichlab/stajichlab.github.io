A roundup of papers from the middle and later part of 2025 with lab members and collaborators.

## Fungal pathogens and antifungal resistance

- **[Giant transposons promote strain heterogeneity in a major fungal pathogen](https://doi.org/10.1128/mbio.01092-25)** (*mBio*). Emile Gluck-Thaler, Robert Cramer, Aaron Vogan, and colleagues, including Jason Stajich, analyzed 519 *Aspergillus fumigatus* strains and found 20 distinct giant transposons that generate genomic heterogeneity, mobilizing genes with roles in biofilm-related virulence and biosynthetic gene clusters.
- **[Coordinated regulation of Mdr1- and Cdr1-mediated protection from antifungals by the Mrr1 transcription factor in emerging *Candida* species](https://doi.org/10.1128/mbio.01323-25)** (*mBio*). With Deborah Hogan's lab at Dartmouth and [Carolina Piña Páez](/members/carolina-pina-paez), this work used CUT&RUN to define the Mrr1 regulon in *Candida lusitaniae*, showing that Mrr1 coordinates two multi-drug transporters and identifying a conserved binding motif.
- **[Morphological and genetic characterization of novel *Sarocladium spinificis* strains in association with *Coccidioides posadasii*](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.03.31.646452)** (bioRxiv preprint). A collaboration with Bridget Barker's lab on a *Sarocladium* strain that suppresses *Coccidioides* growth.
- **[Comparative Genomics Reveals Intra and Inter Species Variation in the Pathogenic Fungus *Batrachochytrium dendrobatidis*](https://doi.org/10.1093/gbe/evaf114)** (*Genome Biology and Evolution*). From [Mark Yacoub](/members/mark-yacoub)'s PhD work on the amphibian pathogen.

## Chytrid cell biology

- **[Dynamic remodeling of centrioles and the microtubule cytoskeleton in the lifecycle of chytrid fungi](https://doi.org/10.1091/mbc.E24-12-0577)** (*Molecular Biology of the Cell*). With Tim Stearns's and Lillian Fritz-Laylin's groups, this study uses RNA-sequencing and expansion microscopy to show how centrioles and the microtubule cytoskeleton are remodeled as chytrids grow from zoospore to multinucleate coenocyte.

## Extreme environments, soils, and microbiomes

- **[Increased aridity is associated with diversity and composition changes in the biocrust mycobiome](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.03.05.641731)** (bioRxiv preprint). [Kian Kelly](/members/kian-kelly), [Xin-zhan Liu](/members/xinzhan-liu), and colleagues examine how aridity shapes fungal communities in biological soil crusts.
- **[Draft genome sequence of an uncultured archaeon from Antarctic endolithic communities](https://doi.org/10.1128/mra.00427-25)** (*Microbiology Resource Announcements*). [Claudia Coleine](/members/claudia-coleine) and colleagues.
- **[Soil drivers of fungal, bacterial and plant diversity in contaminated Southern Californian sites: Implications for dryland bioremediation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scitotenv.2025.180357)** (*Science of the Total Environment*). A survey of seven contaminated Southern California sites with [Tania Kurbessoian](/members/tania-kurbessoian), which identifies native plants that accumulate copper and soil factors that shape microbial communities, as candidates for dryland bioremediation.
- **[Celebrating the fifth edition of the International Symposium on Fungal Stress (ISFUS)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.funbio.2025.101590)** (*Fungal Biology*).

## Genomes, tools, and data

- **[Kingdom-wide CRISPR guide design with ALLEGRO](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkaf783)** (*Nucleic Acids Research*). A UC Riverside collaboration with Ian Wheeldon's and Stefano Lonardi's labs and [Xin-zhan Liu](/members/xinzhan-liu). ALLEGRO uses integer linear programming to design compact sgRNA libraries targeting over 2,000 fungal species, with edits confirmed in four yeasts.
- **[A roadmap for equitable reuse of public microbiome data](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41564-025-02116-2)** (*Nature Microbiology*). Proposes a Data Reuse Information (DRI) tag tied to an ORCID so data creators can ask to be contacted before reuse.
- **[A haplotype-resolved chromatin landscape connects cis-regulatory variants to trait variation in *Citrus*](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12864-025-12137-0)** (*BMC Genomics*). With Danelle Seymour's and Susan Wessler's labs, including [Talieh Ostovar](/members/talieh-ostovar) and [Jinfeng Chen](/members/jinfeng-chen). A locally phased mandarin genome links haplotype-specific chromatin states to allele-specific gene expression and fruit traits.
- **[Draft genome of *Conoideocrella luteorostrata* ARSEF 14590](https://doi.org/10.1128/mra.01273-24)** and **[Genome annotation of *Aspergillus melleus* strain CBS 546.65](https://doi.org/10.1128/mra.00519-25)** (*Microbiology Resource Announcements*). Two genome announcements.
- **[Activation of the Trichodimerol Pathway through Deletion of *mcrA* in Marine *Penicillium rubens* YAP001](https://doi.org/10.1021/acschembio.4c00761)** (*ACS Chemical Biology*). With Clay Wang's lab at USC, activating a secondary metabolite pathway to produce sorbicillinoids and trichodimerol.
